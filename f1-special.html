THE PEOPLE AND THE ENGINEERING

INSIDE THE PADDOCK

TEAM CULTURE

Constructors & Garages

Formula 1 teams consist of engineers, designers, mechanics, strategists and drivers. They work together to design, develop and maintain racing cars. Every team aims to improve performance, reliability and race results.

RACE WEEKEND

Practice to Grand Prix

A Formula 1 race weekend usually includes practice sessions, qualifying and the main Grand Prix. Practice helps teams test their cars, qualifying determines the starting positions, and the race decides the final results.

RACECRAFT

Strategy & Pit Stops

Race strategy involves selecting tyres, planning pit stops and adapting to weather and track conditions. Teams use real-time information to make decisions that can improve a driver's position and overall performance.

PRECISION ENGINEERING

THE TECHNOLOGY OF SPEED

AERODYNAMICS

Airflow & Downforce

Aerodynamics is the study of how air moves around a Formula 1 car. Front wings, rear wings and bodywork help generate downforce, which improves grip and stability while managing air resistance.

POWER UNIT

Hybrid Engineering

A Formula 1 power unit combines an internal combustion engine with electrical energy systems. It converts fuel and recovered energy into power, helping the car achieve high performance and efficiency.

TYRE MANAGEMENT

Grip & Degradation

Tyres provide the grip needed for acceleration, braking and cornering. Their performance changes with temperature, driving style and track conditions. Teams carefully manage tyre wear to maintain consistent performance.

SAFETY

Driver Protection

Safety is a major priority in Formula 1. Features such as the survival cell, Halo device, protective barriers, fire-resistant clothing and strict safety procedures help protect drivers during racing incidents.
