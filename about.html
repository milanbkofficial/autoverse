ABOUT VYROX MOTORS

VYROX MOTORS is an automotive discovery platform created for people who are curious about cars and motorsport. Explore vehicle brands and categories, compare key specifications, learn about performance and technology, and discover a dedicated Formula 1 experience.

FOUNDER & CREATOR

Milan BK

VYROX MOTORS is an independent automotive digital project founded by Milan BK, built around a passion for automobiles, performance engineering and motorsport.

The platform brings together car discovery, detailed vehicle information, side-by-side comparisons and motorsport exploration in one place. Its goal is to make discovering and understanding automobiles a more engaging, informative and accessible experience.

A note from the founder: “VYROX MOTORS started with a simple idea: to create a platform that brings the automotive world closer to enthusiasts. Built through curiosity, creativity and continuous learning, this project represents my interest in automotive technology, design and innovation. This is just the beginning of the journey.”
